# Dispatch 
The dispatch process begins once all scopes and work tasks in a service request have been released and ready for assignment 

There are three ways the dispatching works
1. [[Manual Resource allocation and monitoring ]] (By the dispatchers)
2. [[Scheduling integration]] (Automated)
3. [[Appointment Booking ]] (By Customers)

*These sub-process run independently but converge to ensure task are assigned properly*

- The Service management widgets provide operational insights 
- The outcome of these process are Work Assignments in released Status

## Benefits
- Improved Customer satisfaction 
- Right Technician for the right task
- Operational Efficiency (Balance Technician workload and Maintains SLA Contract)
